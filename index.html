Why Choose Hello Teeth?

Dental care that continues
after your procedure.

At Hello Teeth, our care continues even after your dental procedure is completed. Patients undergoing dental surgery receive seven days of post-surgery dietary guidance from our dietitian. This personalized support helps patients understand what to eat, what to avoid, and how to maintain proper nutrition during recovery. By combining expert dental treatment with attentive aftercare, we aim to make recovery safer, smoother, and more comfortable.

Post-surgery nutrition care

Dr. Anjali Shukla

Dietitian · 8 years of experience

With eight years of professional experience, Dr. Anjali Shukla provides personalized nutritional guidance to help patients recover comfortably after dental surgery. She works closely with the Hello Teeth dental team to prepare practical diet plans based on each patient’s procedure, health requirements, and recovery stage.

PRIVACY & INQUIRIES

A thoughtful first hello.

This website does not save inquiry information in your browser. When available, the inquiry form is hosted by Google Forms, and your responses are shared with the clinic through its Google account.

Only provide basic contact details and a general reason for your inquiry. Do not include sensitive health information, medical records, identity documents, or payment details.

The clinic can use the details you submit to respond to your inquiry. Sending a form does not confirm an appointment. Contact the clinic for questions about your response or to request deletion.

Google-hosted forms and fonts make requests to Google’s servers. Google’s privacy policy applies to its services.
