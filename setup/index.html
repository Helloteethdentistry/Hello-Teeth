FOR THE CLINIC OWNER

A little setup.
A warmer first hello.

Your patient inquiry form is ready to create. Run the prepared script once in your Google account to create the form and its response spreadsheet, then connect it to your website.

Account setup required · the Google Form has not been created yet
01

Open your Google workspace

Use the Google account that will own the clinic’s inquiries. Create a new Apps Script project and name it Hello Teeth — Inquiry Setup.

Open Google Apps Script ↗
02

Let the script build your form

Copy the script below. Replace everything in the project’s Code.gs file, then save. Select createHelloTeethInquiryForm from the function menu and click Run.

Google will ask you to sign in and review access to Forms and Sheets. This runs in your account. No website server, deployment, or API key is needed.

Download .gs file

Read the complete script

You can also select this text and copy it manually. The downloadable file contains the same script.

03

Find your form and patient tracker

The Apps Script execution log prints three links. Save the form editor and patient inquiries sheet links for your team. Copy the PUBLIC FORM URL for your website.

You can also find both files in your Google Drive by searching for Hello Teeth.

Open the public form in a private browser window. Check that responder access is set to Anyone with the link and submit a test inquiry. Confirm its arrival in the linked spreadsheet. A managed Google Workspace account may need an administrator to allow external responders.

04

Connect it to your website

Paste your public form link below to prepare the setting. Then replace the empty googleFormUrl value in clinic-config.js with this URL, save the file, and refresh your website.

Use the public responder link ending in /viewform. If you have a forms.gle short link, open it and copy the full address.

Open your form ↗

This helper prepares text only; it does not change your project files. Keep the form editor and response spreadsheet URLs out of your website configuration.

YOUR INQUIRY FORM

Every conversation
starts with care.

A thoughtful, short form for patients near and far.

  • Name and email address
  • Optional international phone number
  • Country of residence
  • Area of interest
  • Preferred contact method
  • Optional visit timing and message
  • Permission to contact
✦ Replies appear in your Google Sheet.

One setup. Your account.

Run the script again in the same project to retrieve the existing links. It reuses the form and spreadsheet, preserves responses, and sends no emails.

The form asks for a general inquiry and basic contact details. Patients are asked to leave out medical records and payment information.

MAKE IT YOURS

The finishing touches

In the form editor, use Customize theme to add the clinic logo, set an evergreen accent, and preview the form on your phone. In clinic-config.js, add the clinic’s real phone number, email, address, and dentist’s name before publishing the website.

This setup uses Google’s Forms service and Spreadsheet service. The clinic website remains entirely front end.
